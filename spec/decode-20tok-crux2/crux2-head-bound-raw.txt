=== the exclusion bound ===
  max|W[t,j]| <= max|svh| * max|suh| * max|q| = 6.082031 * 0.172119 * 3.625000 = 3.794773

  Let L = SUM_j |h_j| * |suh[j]| * max|q|  (the per-row bound factor, shared by all rows
  up to the row's own svh). Then the bound on the UNREAD part is  svh[t] * L.
  config.json: final_logit_softcapping = 30.0  =>  |logit_t| <= 30 for EVERY token.

  For the scheme to exclude even one token it needs   svh[t] * L < 30   for that token,
  i.e. a token can only be excluded when its own row scale is below 30 / L.
  L in turn is at least  max|suh| * max|q| * (something >= 1).

  Lower bound on L with no knowledge of h at all: L >= max|suh| * max|q| * min_j |h_j|*...

=== threshold ===
  required  SUM_j |h_j|*|suh[j]|  <  30 / (svh[t] * max|q|)  for token t to be excludable
  median |svh| = 0.850586  ->  threshold = 9.730
  max    |svh| = 6.082031  ->  threshold = 1.361

  With the usual post-RMSNorm |h_j| ~ 1, SUM_j |h_j||suh[j]| is of order
  rms|suh| * h = 0.015341 * 5376 = 82.475
  => the bound is about 8.5x the post-RMSNorm estimate of that sum, i.e. 8x the softcap.

=== how much of the head may be SKIPPED? ===
  SUM_j |suh[j]|  = 81.359   (real checkpoint data)
  L1_all = SUM_j |h_j||suh[j]| ~ E|h| * SUM|suh| = 0.7979 * 81.359 = 64.916
  full-head bound B_t = |svh| * C * L1_all = 0.8506 * 3.6250 * 64.916 = 200.2
  softcap on ANY logit = 30.0  =>  the full-head bound is 6.7x the softcap

  necessary condition to exclude the median-scale row: unread L1 mass < 9.730
  => at most 14.99% of the head's columns may be left unread
  => MAXIMUM bytes any EXACT two-stage head could ever save = 0.1584 GB of 1.0570 GB
  (for the largest row scale |svh|=6.0820: at most 2.10% skippable)
