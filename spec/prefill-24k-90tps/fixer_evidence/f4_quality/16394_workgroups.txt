# The Architecture of Reality: An Explanation of Einstein’s Theory of Relativity

The theory of relativity revolutionized our understanding of space, time, and the universe. It fundamentally challenges the Newtonian view of the universe, asserting that the laws of physics are the same for all observers, regardless of their motion. The theory is divided into two parts: Special Relativity and General Relativity.

### 1. Special Relativity (1905)
Special Relativity deals with the relationship between space and time. The core idea is that the laws of physics remain the same regardless of the motion of the observer. This leads to two fundamental concepts:

*   **Time Dilation:** The idea that time passes slower for an observer moving relative to a second observer. This is a consequence of the fact that the speed of light is constant.
*   **Length Contraction:** The idea that a moving clock will appear shorter than a stationary clock.

The theory also states that the speed of light is constant regardless of the motion of the observer. This is the fundamental premise of the theory. The theory also explains that mass and energy are directly proportional. The most famous equation from this theory is $E = mc^2$.
